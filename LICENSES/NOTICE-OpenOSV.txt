(https://github.com/Kemerd/OpenOSV), in src/core/DlogM.h and
src/shaders/dlogm.slang, under the Apache License 2.0 (LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt):
the Osmo 360 D-Log M curve and native primaries matrix, from commit
3a39776272efb5dfdc1d29711ae746e855383084, and the Avata 360 D-Log M curve and
native primaries matrix, from commit 343e77eb4c1076d08b0854a23884c7b91f8197de
(OpenOSV pull request #3, by Kemerd and the OpenOSV contributors). The parts
of OpenOSV's NOTICE that pertain to them follow, verbatim, the Avata 360
bullet from the later commit. Paths inside it are OpenOSV's. The first bullet
describes a curve this program does not use; it is kept because the second
bullet's "of the same form" refers to it. Of the Avata 360 bullet, this
program uses the curve and the matrix, not the look.

* The "DJI-matched" D-Log M decoding curve (kDlogMDjiRefit, `--fit dji`) is an
  analytic 7-parameter fit produced by scripts/fit_dlogm.py from 64
  neutral-axis measurements of DJI's publicly distributed D-Log M to HLG
  rendering (the FT_StyleGeneralDlogm2HLG style LUT bundled with DJI Studio).
  What is shipped is the fitted constants (include/osv/color/DlogM.h) and the
  64-value measurement table, recorded as the fit's provenance
  (scripts/fit_dlogm.py) and as the tests' reference
  (tests/unit/test_color.cpp); no DJI LUT data beyond those 64 values is
  included.
* The "Osmo 360" D-Log M decoding curve (kDlogMOsmo360, the default) is an
  analytic 7-parameter fit of the same form, produced by
  `scripts/fit_dlogm.py --from-cube DJI_Osmo360_DLogM_to_Rec709.cube` from the
  33 neutral-axis (R == G == B) samples of DJI's publicly distributed Osmo 360
  D-Log M to Rec.709 LUT (as bundled with DJI Studio).  Measuring a transfer
  function is what was done, not copying a table: what is shipped is the
  seven fitted constants (include/osv/color/DlogM.h) and the 33 measured
  diagonal values, recorded as the tests' reference
  (tests/unit/DjiReference.h).  The fit scripts read the LUT from a local
  copy (DJI Studio installs one) and print only fitted constants.
* The Osmo 360 native primaries matrix (kNativeToRec2020_Osmo360, the
  default) is OpenOSV's least-squares fit, produced by
  `scripts/fit_primaries.py --from-cube DJI_Osmo360_DLogM_to_Rec709.cube`
  from measurements of all 35937 entries of the same LUT.  Only the nine
  fitted constants are shipped (include/osv/color/Matrices.h).
* The "Avata 360" D-Log M decoding curve (kDlogMAvata360, `--fit avata360`),
  its native primaries matrix (kNativeToRec2020_Avata360) and its Rec.709
  look (kLookDjiRec709Avata360) are OpenOSV's least-squares fits, produced by
  scripts/fit_dlogm.py, scripts/fit_primaries.py and scripts/fit_look.py
  from measurements of DJI's publicly distributed Avata 360 D-Log M to
  Rec.709 LUT (as bundled with DJI Studio), exactly as for the Osmo 360.
  Only the fitted constants are shipped (include/osv/color/DlogM.h,
  include/osv/color/Matrices.h, include/osv/color/Look.h); no DJI LUT data is
  redistributed, and the Avata 360 .cube files in luts/ are generated by
  `osvtool lut` from the fitted model.  The first Avata 360 curve and matrix
  were contributed by an OpenOSV contributor, from their own footage.
